About BYU Athletics

Brigham Young University's athletic program, the Cougars, competes in the Big 12 Conference. Known for their passionate fan base and a rich tradition of excellence, BYU Athletics represents the university's values of hard work, integrity, and teamwork across more than 20 varsity sports.

Sports at BYU

BYU competes in a wide range of sports. Here are some highlights:

  1. Football
    • Member of the Big 12 Conference
    • Multiple top-25 national rankings
    • Home games at LaVell Edwards Stadium
  2. Basketball (Men's)
    • Regular NCAA Tournament appearances
    • Jimmer Fredette — 2011 National Player of the Year
    • Plays at the Marriott Center
  3. Baseball
    • Consistent postseason contender
    • Produced numerous MLB draft picks
  4. Cross Country / Track & Field
    • Multiple WCC and Big 12 titles
    • Nationally ranked programs
